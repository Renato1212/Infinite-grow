## Why this matters

A hypothesis is a path; an opportunity is a tradeable location on it.

The asymmetry score — potential ticks × your estimated probability — exists to keep
you honest about which of these is actually worth the risk. You have said you want
only the most directional, fastest moves born of multi-position liquidation and
informational catalysts. Score the opportunities and the chop tends to fall to the
bottom of the list on its own.
