## Why this matters

The 30-minute chart routine, captured in a shape that survives.

`structure_note` and `ladder_behaviour` are prose because they are genuinely
narrative. Everything beside them — VWAP slope, prior day type, expected range,
bias, conviction — is structured because those are the fields you will slice by.
"Do I trade better when I had high conviction and a flat VWAP?" is only
answerable if conviction and slope are columns.

`expected_range_ticks` is the one number here that will embarrass you most
usefully: it is a forecast with an outcome.

**Feeds:** Context.
