## Why this matters

Everything that is true about the day before you have an opinion about it.

Scheduled events, the dynamic calendar, flow flags, options positioning. These are
the conditions your hypotheses live inside. Flagged as booleans rather than buried
in a note, so Study can slice by them: OPEX days, roll days, month-end.

`expected_environment` is your synthesis — the paragraph you would say out loud if
someone asked "what kind of day is this going to be?"

**Feeds:** Context.
