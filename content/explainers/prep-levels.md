## Why this matters

A marked level is a prediction. Recording how price actually traded it turns your
chart mark-up from decoration into a dataset.

Mark the level in the morning. During or after the session, say what happened:
respected, broke, broke and retested, or never touched — and by how many ticks.

After a quarter of this you can answer the question that actually matters: which
level types do you read well, and which ones do you keep drawing because they
look tidy?
