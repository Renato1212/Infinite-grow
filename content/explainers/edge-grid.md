## Why this matters

This is the most valuable data in the app.

For every trade, score all five domains: was each one supportive, neutral,
conflicting, or simply not applicable? How much did it weigh? Which one was
**primary** — the reason you took the trade?

Pre-filled with "not applicable", so an honest scoring takes fifteen seconds.

What it buys you: a direct answer to "which domains actually pay me?" and the
harder one — "what happens to my results when I take a technical setup against a
conflicting central-bank story?" Nobody can tell you that. Only your own data can.

**Feeds:** Edge.
