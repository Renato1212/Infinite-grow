## Why this matters

The three pillars get three separate boxes on purpose. Mixed into one note, they
blur; separated, you can see that your context reads are good and your process is
what keeps failing — or the reverse.

`what_i_saw` versus `what_was_actually_there` is the deliberate-practice core.
Not "was it a winner", but "was my read correct". Those come apart more often than
is comfortable.

`repeatable` is the summary judgment: knowing everything you now know, would you
take this trade again, identically? A losing trade can be repeatable. A winning one
often is not.

**Feeds:** Edge, Process.
