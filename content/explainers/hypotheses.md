## Why this matters

A hypothesis is a **path**, not a direction. "Up" is not a hypothesis. "Trades
back to yesterday's VAH, fails there, rotates to the overnight low" is.

That is why a hypothesis is built from your marked levels in order: the path is
literally a route through the lines you drew.

Rank them. Assign a probability — a real one, that you will be held to. Write the
invalidation before the session, because you will not write an honest one during it.

At debrief you record what actually happened: played out, partial, invalidated,
never triggered. That single field is what makes your forecasting measurable rather
than remembered.

**Feeds:** Context, Edge, Process.
